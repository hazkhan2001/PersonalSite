This is a template for a book review. It is marked `draft: true`, so it stays hidden.

The `book`, `author`, and `rating` fields at the top add a "A review of ... by ..." line and stars under the title. To link a review from the Books page, put this file's name (without `.md`) in the book's `review:` field in `content/data/books.yaml`.
